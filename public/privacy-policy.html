ESUG 2027 App — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy applies to the ESUG 2027 conference app for Android and iOS, and to the same app in a web browser. The app shows information about the ESUG conference (programme, talks, venue, city, travel, registration) and about past ESUG conferences.

In short

The app does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Some content is loaded from YouTube, as described below.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or enter personal data.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking services.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. The only permission the Android app requests is internet access.
  • It does not store information about you on your device or on any server.

Content loaded from YouTube

The conference information is bundled with the app. The exception is video: when you open a talk that has a recording, the app shows a preview image loaded from YouTube (img.youtube.com). In a web browser, the video player is embedded using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Loading this content sends your device's IP address and standard browser information to YouTube (Google), as with any image or video on the web. We do not receive this information. Google's handling of it is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Links to other websites

The app contains links to other websites, such as YouTube, presentation slides, the conference registration site, and travel and accommodation providers. These open in your browser or in another app, and their own privacy policies apply.

Web version

The web version of the app is hosted on Amazon Web Services (Amazon S3 and CloudFront). To deliver the pages, AWS necessarily processes your IP address. We have not enabled access logging, so we do not keep records of visits.

Children

The app is intended for conference attendees and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Because the app does not collect or hold personal data about you, there is no data for us to give you access to, correct or delete. If you have questions or concerns, please contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
